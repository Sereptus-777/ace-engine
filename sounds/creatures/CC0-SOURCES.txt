ACE Engine — Creature SFX provenance
====================================

All sound files in these creature folders that are prefixed with "fs_"
were sourced from Freesound (https://freesound.org) and are licensed
under Creative Commons 0 (CC0 1.0 / Public Domain Dedication).

CC0 means:
  - No rights reserved. Free for commercial use.
  - No attribution required.
  - Safe to redistribute as part of a paid module.

Filenames encode the Freesound sound ID:  fs_<id>_<name>.mp3
Full provenance (id, original name, author, duration) is in:
  creature-sound-manifest.csv

Files were fetched as Freesound "HQ MP3" quality via the Freesound API,
filtered to license:"Creative Commons 0".

Note: any files in these folders WITHOUT the "fs_" prefix pre-existed and
were not added by this process — verify their licensing separately before
shipping.
