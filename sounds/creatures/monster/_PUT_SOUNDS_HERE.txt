GENERIC CREATURE SOUNDS — the catch-all bucket.

Anything dropped here plays for a creature whose own family folder is empty.
Keep clips SHORT (under 2.5s — longer ones are cut off) and dry (no music,
no reverb tails, no ambience).

Accepted: .mp3  .wav  .ogg  .flac  .webm

⚠️ FILENAMES MATTER. Anything containing walking, footstep, step, ambien,
loop, electric, cartoon, robotic, sci-fi, singing or chirp is IGNORED on
purpose — those are not vocalisations. Name clips for the noise itself:
   roar-01.mp3   growl-deep.wav   snarl-short.ogg   grunt-heavy.mp3

New files are picked up within 2 minutes (or reload).
