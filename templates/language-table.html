What does a foreign tongue sound like out loud?

Chat text is hidden from anyone who cannot read the language. Audio cannot be hidden the same way — so when an NPC speaks a tongue you do not know, the voice says it in a real-world stand-in instead of reading it aloud in English. The chat log still carries the English meaning, so a character who genuinely knows the language can read it.
